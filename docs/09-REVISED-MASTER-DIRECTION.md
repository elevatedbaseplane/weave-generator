## Purpose

Weave Generator is an upstream architectural authoring tool. The designer authors two directional systems and their interactions, reads the conditions they produce, and develops selected relationships into source geometry for Tangent and Overlap.

Boundary/context → authored carrier and influences → weave interactions → measured conditions → analytical markings and editable spatial readings → curated points → relations/polylines → Tangent forms → Overlap relationships and residual spaces.

The analytical drawing is a useful independent output. The complete purpose also includes deliberate closed-form composition. Seam/interface tracing is the first focused route into that purpose, not a replacement for broader point extraction.

## Retained foundation and scope

Preserve Phase 1A/1B/2A: document coordinates, validated/versioned boundaries, supported straight SVG boundary import, rectangular A/B carriers, analytical clipping, stable source keys, immutable Carrier Studies, undo, browser-local persistence and JSON backups. Do not redefine density, perpendicular A/B geometry, or saved rect-v1 derivation to implement new behavior.

Retain the broader roadmap: authored deformation; two source families; explicit over/under, binding, release and routing; meaningful point curation; closed polylines; separate geometry exports; source ancestry; Tangent for formal differentiation and Overlap for relational/residual-space exploration. Reference underlays and richer carrier/field types remain future capabilities.

## Settled conceptual contracts

1. Threads are abstract directional systems. Architectural roles may be assigned per study; geometry does not infer program, floors, circulation, or privacy.
2. Operations change geometry or relationships. Analytical stitch markings report detected conditions. Placing or hiding a marking never binds, reroutes, or disconnects threads.
3. Geometric intersections, over/under precedence, and connected junctions are separate facts. A rendering gap for an underpass is not a physical opening or a break in source continuity.
4. Measurements remain factual within a declared rule. Spatial readings are editable interpretations. Compression can support a seam or buffer reading without its measured distances changing.
5. Conditions may occupy a point, interval, interface, or region, and may overlap. Keep the full analysis separate from display and curated selection limits.
6. Geometric, behavioral, and influence-based interfaces are distinct condition types. Their evidence must not be merged into an unexplained generic score.
7. A narrow band can have a center trace; a boundary between regions has a contact trace. Extraction must name which one it produces. No automatic claim that a center trace is a separating wall.
8. Points come from source conditions through an explicit extraction rule, never from symbol artwork. Every point and relation retains its reason and source revision. Manual additions remain identifiable.
9. Branches produce separate open traces terminating at a shared node. Closed output requires an explicit region/band construction or an already closed valid relation cycle; never connect arbitrary endpoints just to satisfy an exporter.
10. A point carries measurements and an optional curvature intention. A numerical corner radius belongs to a particular ordered polyline and receiving geometry. No universal radius is assigned solely by symbol type.

## Optional exploration and postponement

Retain independently switchable multi-scale readings, contextual interpretations, symbol appearance controls, selectable evidence, interface previews, construction view, and saved-study comparison. Start with evidence and trace previews; comparison waits for complete saved Weave studies.

Postpone general cell topology, branched region composition, the full Atlas vocabulary, imported field zones, triangular/radial carriers, field links, automatic radius application, rich receiver metadata, native DWG, and open-polyline receiver support until their specific contracts are settled. They are not dependencies of C1–C3.

Literal textile physics, automatic architectural judgment/building generation, unlimited source families, and live cross-tool synchronization remain outside the direction.

## How to judge this direction

Every delivered example must explain: what was authored; what happened; why a marking appeared; what its interpretation adds; why points were extracted; why connections exist; and what was preserved or changed downstream. Visual appeal alone is insufficient, and measured geometry alone does not establish architectural success.
